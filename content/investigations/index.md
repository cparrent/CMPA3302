# Investigations

Criminal investigations combine information from people, physical evidence and digital sources to determine what occurred during an incident. Technology gives investigators additional tools for collecting, organizing and analyzing information, but those tools must be used alongside sound investigative practices.

Modern investigations may involve electronic devices, recorded interviews, crime data and information obtained from other law enforcement systems.
