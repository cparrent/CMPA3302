# Automated License Plate Readers

Automated license plate readers (ALPRs) use cameras and software to capture license plate information from passing vehicles. These systems can be mounted on patrol vehicles or installed at fixed locations. The plate information can then be compared with law enforcement databases or stored according to agency policy.

## Law Enforcement Uses

ALPR systems can help officers identify vehicles associated with stolen vehicle reports, wanted persons or other legitimate law enforcement purposes. Instead of requiring an officer to manually enter every license plate, the system can scan multiple plates and notify the officer when information matches a designated database.

Information obtained from an ALPR can also support [[crime-analysis|Crime Analysis]] by helping investigators identify vehicle patterns or determine whether a vehicle was detected in a particular area.

## Alerts and Verification

An ALPR alert should not automatically be treated as proof that a crime has occurred. Information in databases can be outdated, plates can be misread and the driver of a vehicle may not be the vehicle's registered owner.

Officers should verify an alert and consider the circumstances before taking enforcement action. The technology is most useful as an investigative tool that provides additional information for an officer to evaluate.

## Privacy and Legal Considerations

Because ALPR systems can collect information about the locations of vehicles, their use creates privacy and data-retention concerns. Agencies should establish policies addressing who may access the information, how long records are retained and the purposes for which searches may be conducted.

These issues also connect ALPR technology with [[search-and-seizure|Search and Seizure]], particularly as courts and lawmakers continue to address privacy questions involving modern technology.

Automated license plate readers can help officers locate vehicles and develop investigative leads, but responsible use requires accurate information, verification and appropriate safeguards.
