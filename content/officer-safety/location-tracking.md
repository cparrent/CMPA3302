# Location Tracking and Officer Safety

Knowing where officers are located can be extremely important during law enforcement operations. Location-tracking technology can provide dispatchers and other officers with additional information about the location of patrol units. This information becomes particularly valuable when an officer needs assistance or when multiple units are responding to the same incident.

## Location and Situational Awareness

Modern patrol vehicles may use GPS technology to provide location information. Combined with computer-aided dispatch systems, this can help dispatchers understand which units are near a call and coordinate available resources. Location information can also reduce reliance on an officer verbally describing a position during a rapidly changing incident.

This creates a close relationship between location tracking and [[communication-technology|communication technology]]. A location on a screen has limited value unless that information can be communicated and understood by the people responding to an incident.

> Technology is most valuable to officer safety when it improves situational awareness without replacing an officer's judgment.

## Connection to Patrol Vehicles

Location systems are also closely associated with [[vehicle-technology|patrol vehicle technology]]. GPS receivers, navigation systems and mobile computers can provide officers with directions and location information while responding to calls.

### Limitations

Location data should be viewed as another source of information rather than a perfect representation of an officer's circumstances. GPS accuracy, equipment problems or delays in updating information can affect what is displayed. Officers and dispatchers therefore still need clear radio communication and awareness of their surroundings.
