# Criminal Law

Criminal law establishes the legal framework within which law enforcement officers perform their duties. Officers must understand constitutional protections, legal standards and evidentiary requirements when conducting investigations, making arrests and using force.

Modern technology creates additional legal considerations because officers can now collect and access information in ways that were not possible in the past. Understanding how established legal principles apply to new technology is an important part of modern policing.
